How it works: The opening is filled by a sliding door between two fixed sidelights. Panels overlap at each meeting stile. When the door slides open behind the right sidelight, the walk-through is the gap between the sidelights, minus any projection.
Formulas: Opening = Left SL + Door + Right SL − Left OL − Right OL  |  Walk-through = Opening − Left SL − Right SL  |  Projection = max(Projection input, Door − Right SL)  |  Clear Opening = Walk-through − Projection
