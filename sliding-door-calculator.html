How it works: The total opening is filled by two sidelights (fixed panels) and a sliding door, which overlap at each meeting stile. When the door slides open behind the right sidelight, the walk-through is the clear gap between the two sidelights. The projection is how far the door extends past the sidelight into the walk-through when open.
Formulas: Opening = 2 × Sidelight + Door − 2 × Overlap  |  Walk-through = Opening − 2 × Sidelight  |  Clear Opening = Walk-through − Projection
